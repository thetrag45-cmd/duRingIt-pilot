§ 1 — What it is

duRingIt is a self-knowledge tool, not a financial advisor.

We help you understand how you actually behave with money — through one short quiz and 11 distinct Monie ID archetypes. We don't tell you what to invest in, what to save, or what to spend on. We hand you a mirror, and the rest is up to you.

The Monie ID system describes patterns you already have. Drive, restraint, optimism, vigilance, avoidance. Built well, it shows you where your strengths quietly come from — and where the same instincts can quietly cost you.

§ 2 — Why we built it

Most money advice ignores the human in the equation.

You can read a thousand articles about index funds, emergency funds, and 4% withdrawal rates, and still not act on a single one. Knowledge isn't the bottleneck — behaviour is.

We built duRingIt because the questions we most wanted answered were never what should I do, but why do I keep doing this. Why does one person fear running out of money even with millions saved, while another keeps spending in their 40s the way they did in their 20s? The Monie ID system is one small attempt to make those patterns legible.

§ 3 — How the quiz works

16 questions. ~4 minutes. No spreadsheets.

The quiz has three short stages:

  • 12 multiple-choice questions — about how you think and decide.
  • 4 quick 1-to-5 scales — about how you actually behave under pressure.
  • An optional tie-breaker — if your top two scores are close, three quick prompts disambiguate.

If two archetypes still tie after the tie-breaker, you become a Bridge — a rare reading where two patterns genuinely co-exist in you.

§ 4 — Is this science?

Honest answer: it's a pilot framework.

The Monie ID system draws on patterns from behavioural finance and personality research — but it isn't peer-reviewed, it isn't a medical instrument, and it isn't a diagnosis. Treat your result as a mirror, not a verdict.

If something resonates, sit with it. If something feels off, that itself is useful information about how you see yourself.

We're actively validating the framework. The feedback screen at the end of the quiz lets you rate accuracy and add notes — that data helps us improve.

§ 5 — Privacy & data

What we collect, what we don't.

No accounts. No tracking pixels. No third-party analytics.

Your quiz answers and result live only in your browser, in sessionStorage. Closing the tab usually clears them. We don't have a server-side database storing your responses.

On the feedback screen at the end of the quiz, you can manually copy your pilot data as JSON and send it to the team. That export only happens when you copy and paste it — we don't pull it automatically.

If you decide to provide your email through a future report form, we'll store only what's needed to send you that report. We won't sell it, ever.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Their servers may log your IP as part of standard CDN delivery. We don't access those logs.
