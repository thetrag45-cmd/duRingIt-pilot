Common questions

Quick answers

Is the quiz really 4 minutes?
Most people finish in 3 to 5 minutes. There are 16 questions: 12 multiple choice and 4 quick 1-to-5 scales. No essays, no math, no sign-up wall.
What if I'm between two types?
If your top two scores are close, the quiz runs a short Identity Calibration to choose between them. If they tie even after that, you become a Bridge — someone whose money behaviour genuinely operates on two tracks. Both card profiles will apply to you.
Is this real psychology or just for fun?
It's a pilot framework — built around behavioural-finance patterns you'd recognise from everyday life. The archetypes describe tendencies, not diagnoses. Treat your result as a mirror, not a verdict.
Do you store my answers?
No accounts, no tracking. Your result lives in your browser. You can copy and share your pilot data manually from the feedback screen if you'd like to send it back to the team.
Can I retake the quiz?
Yes — there's a "Start over" link on the feedback screen, or just reload and tap "Open my intake file" again.
